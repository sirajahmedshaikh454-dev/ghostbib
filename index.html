Ghostbib

Paste your reference list. Ghostbib checks every reference against Crossref and shows which fields match a real paper: title, authors, year and DOI.

🔒 Each reference is sent to Crossref, a public scholarly database, and nowhere else.

Results

How to read the results, and what Ghostbib cannot tell you
  • Verified: a real record matches the title, the first author and the year.
  • Authors differ: a paper with this title exists, but your first author is not one of its authors. This is the classic AI-made fake: a real title with invented authors.
  • Year differs: title and authors match but the record has another date. A typo, or a preprint or republication.
  • DOI is a different paper / does not exist: the DOI in your reference points elsewhere or nowhere.
  • Not found: nothing matches. It may be invented, or it may be a book, thesis, report, standard or web page that Crossref does not index. Search for those yourself before deciding.
  • It proves a paper exists, not that it says what you claim. Read what you cite.
Data: Crossref REST API. Matching runs in your browser.
